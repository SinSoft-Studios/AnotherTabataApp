Privacy Policy – Another Tabata App

Effective date: October 15, 2026  |  Last updated: October 15, 2026

SinSoft Studios ("we", "us", or "our") develops Another Tabata App, a workout interval timer. This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to the app; the website hosting this policy and the store where you obtain the app are separate services.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app works without an account or a SinSoft Studios server. It stores the following information locally on your device to provide its features:

  • Saved workout names, interval descriptions, durations, and rounds.
  • Your selected workout, appearance, sound, volume, and alert preferences.
  • Active workout state so the timer can recover when you reopen the app.
  • Notification activation history and the last observed permission status.

We do not receive this information. The app does not provide cloud sync or send your workout library to us. Device backup or restore features, if enabled by your operating system, are controlled separately through your device and account settings.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The current app does not include live advertising, analytics, or a third-party crash-reporting service. The banner area is a placeholder and does not load ads. The app does not use cookies or advertising identifiers for tracking. If these features change, we will update this policy to describe the information involved.

Notifications, Sounds, and Device Features

Optional local workout notifications provide countdown, interval, and completion alerts. On Android, an active-workout service can keep the timer running in the background and show a notification with a Stop action. You can change alert preferences in the app and notification permissions in your device settings.

The app can keep the screen awake during a workout and play packaged audio cues. Optional spoken alerts use your device's text-to-speech engine for short workout cues. These features do not record your voice or require microphone access. System services and installed speech engines are subject to their providers' settings and privacy policies.

Retention and Deletion

Saved workouts and preferences remain in local app storage until changed or removed. You can delete saved workouts in the app. Clearing the app's storage where supported, or uninstalling the app, can remove its local data. Any device backups are managed separately through your operating system or backup provider. We do not hold a server-side copy of your workouts to retrieve or delete.

Contacting Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond to your request and handle follow-up questions. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed to explain the issue. Contact us if you want us to delete support correspondence we hold about you.

Children's Privacy

The app does not request an age or require an account, and does not send locally saved workout information to us. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, please contact us so we can address the request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted at this address with a revised last-updated date.
