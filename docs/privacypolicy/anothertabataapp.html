Privacy Policy – Another Tabata App

Effective date: October 15, 2026  |  Last updated: October 15, 2026

SinSoft Studios ("we", "us", or "our") develops Another Tabata App, a workout interval timer intended for adults. We are responsible for the app's data practices described here. For privacy questions or requests, contact sinsoftstudios@gmail.com.

Advertising rollout: Google AdMob advertising is planned. Versions that contain only an ad placeholder do not request ads. The advertising sections below explain what applies when you use a version with AdMob enabled. Ad consent and privacy controls described for those versions apply when advertising is enabled; they are not features of the placeholder version.

Workout Data and Local Settings

No account is required. Saved workout names, interval descriptions, durations, rounds, appearance and sound preferences, active-workout recovery state, and notification permission history are stored on your device. We do not receive your workout library or provide cloud sync. We do not send workout contents or support emails to Google as ad targeting data. Device backup and restore, if enabled, are managed separately through your operating system and account settings.

Advertising Through Google AdMob

Ad-supported versions use Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner ads and help fund the app. Google Play distributes the Android app; AdMob is the advertising service. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, your choices, and device settings, Google and advertising providers involved in ad delivery may process:

  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location.
  • Advertising ID, app-related identifiers, and other device or account identifiers where available.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, ad impressions, taps, and video views.
  • Technical and diagnostic information about the device, app, and SDK performance.
  • Consent and privacy choices used to determine which advertising processing is permitted.

This information supports ad delivery and measurement, frequency limits, reporting, service performance, and fraud prevention. Where permitted and consented to, it may also support personalized ads. IP-based location does not require GPS access; the timer does not request precise location to show ads. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Advertising can use device identifiers, local storage, and similar technologies. Ad content or pages opened from ads may also use cookies, pixels, or web beacons. Google receives information directly through its SDK; this processing can occur even though we do not operate a workout server. We may receive advertising performance and revenue reports from AdMob.

Personalized Ads and Your Choices

Personalized ads can use information about interests or prior activity. Non-personalized ads use context, such as the app or approximate location, rather than a past-behavior profile. Non-personalized does not mean no data collection: identifiers or storage may still be used for frequency limits, reporting, or fraud prevention, subject to applicable choices and requirements. See Google's explanation of ad types.

In ad-supported versions, we use a consent-management flow to explain relevant purposes and providers and obtain consent where required, including in the European Economic Area (including Norway), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Personalized advertising is enabled only where permitted and with the required consent. Using the app or reading this policy is not consent to personalized advertising.

Where required, the ad-supported app provides a Privacy choices entry in settings to revisit, refuse, or withdraw choices. Withdrawal affects future processing and does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Depending on your choices and applicable requirements, you may receive non-personalized or limited ads, or no ads. Refusing ad personalization does not disable the workout timer.

Android settings also offer advertising-ID controls; available options and paths vary by device. Google's My Ad Center offers additional controls for eligible Google advertising. Device or Google-account controls do not replace the app's consent choices.

Who Receives Advertising Information

Google and the advertising providers participating in an ad request may receive advertising information. Where a consent message is shown, review its provider list and purposes before choosing. Google processes information under its own policies and applicable advertising terms; it is not simply a storage service for your workout library. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Opening an advertiser's site or app is subject to that provider's privacy policy.

Notifications, Audio, and Other Services

Optional local notifications provide workout alerts. On Android, an active-workout service can display a Stop action while the timer runs in the background. You control notification permission through device settings. The app can keep the screen awake and play packaged sounds; optional spoken cues use the installed text-to-speech engine. These features do not record your voice. Operating-system services and speech providers have their own settings and policies. We do not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting service in the current app; this does not exclude AdMob's advertising measurement and diagnostics in ad-supported versions.

Support and Reasons for Processing

If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this to answer questions, troubleshoot, and handle privacy requests. Please send only information needed for your request. Our email provider also processes correspondence to deliver and store it. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to address abuse or protect legal rights.

Where European data-protection law applies, we rely on consent for advertising personalization and access to device storage where consent is required; our legitimate interests in answering support requests and protecting the app, balanced against your rights; and legal obligations where applicable. Consent requirements take priority over legitimate interests. Google explains its own processing grounds in its privacy policy.

Retention and Deletion

Workouts and preferences remain in local storage until changed or removed. Delete workouts in the app; clearing app storage where supported or uninstalling can remove local app data. Backups are managed separately. We cannot retrieve or delete a server copy of your workout library because we do not hold one.

We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and follow-up, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. You can request deletion by email. Google controls retention of information it processes under its policies; periods vary by data and purpose. See Google's retention explanation. Uninstalling the app, withdrawing consent, or deleting an advertising ID does not automatically erase information already held by advertising providers. Use their privacy request mechanisms for that information, or contact us for help directing a request.

Security and International Processing

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. Google documents encrypted transmission for data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK. Local information also depends on your device's security. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google and other service providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Google describes applicable transfer protections in its data transfer frameworks.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise rights concerning information we hold. We may need proportionate information to verify a request and will respond within applicable deadlines. Some rights have legal exceptions.

Where applicable U.S. state law gives you a right to opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising, use the ad-supported app's Privacy choices controls or contact us. Advertising disclosures can fall within those legal definitions even without a direct sale for money. We do not exchange your saved workout contents or support emails for advertising payments.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support or knowingly enable personalized advertising for children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. An app-store content rating alone does not establish that personalized advertising is appropriate for every user.

Changes and Contact

We will update this page as the app's data practices change, with a revised last-updated date and additional notice or consent where required. For privacy questions, deletion requests, or support, email sinsoftstudios@gmail.com.
